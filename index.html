Built for a smoother workout.

Glide combines a natural elliptical motion with immersive, instructor-led classes so you get a full-body cardio session that's easy on your joints.

↻

Auto-Follow resistance

Resistance and incline adjust automatically to match your instructor's cues. Override any time with the dial.

⇆

Adaptive stride

Stride length adjusts from 18" to 22" so the motion feels natural whatever your height.

▣

24" swivel HD screen

Rotate the screen 180° to move from cardio to strength, yoga, and stretching on the floor.

◎

Whisper-quiet drive

Magnetic resistance and a belt drive keep things quiet enough for early mornings and apartments.

♥

Heart rate zones

Pair any Bluetooth heart rate monitor to train in the right zone, with live on-screen guidance.

✦

Upper-body arms

Moving handlebars engage arms, shoulders, and back. Switch to the fixed grips for a lower-body focus.

Thousands of classes. One low-impact machine.

Live and on-demand Glide classes from 5 to 60 minutes: intervals, endurance, scenic routes, and beginner programs. Off the machine, the screen opens up the full class library.
